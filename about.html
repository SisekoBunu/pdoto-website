About PdotO

Siphelele Mnyande, professionally known as PdotO, is a South African hip-hop artist and songwriter from Pretoria. He is known for his introspective lyricism, consistency, and strong storytelling ability, which has earned him recognition across the South African hip-hop scene.

He began his journey through mixtapes before releasing his debut studio album The Devilz Playground in 2017. His career expanded further with Under the Sun in 2018, while also appearing in theatre productions such as Freedom the Musical and participating in BET Africa Cyphers.

Over the years, PdotO has built a respected catalogue and collaborated with artists like L-Tido, Chad Da Don, Reason, J.Smallz and Blaklez, contributing to his strong presence in the industry.

Discography

  • 2017 - The Devilz Playground
  • 2018 - Under the Sun
  • 2020 - Cold Waters
  • 2020 - Cold Waters Love Eternal
  • 2021 - Cold Waters Low Tides and Lost Tapes
  • 2024 - Siphelele Book 1
  • 2025 - Siphelele Book 2

His upcoming album "Take It As You May" (2026) marks his next chapter, continuing his journey of growth, reflection, and artistic expression.
